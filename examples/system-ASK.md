# system-ASK // one apex, recursive ecology

> **Projection boundary — canonical compression, not system authority.** This file is the maintained
> high-level public projection of `system-ASK` as an ASK-specific worked example. It owns this compression,
> its diagram relation, and its lineage. **It does not own the distributed system it depicts**, and it is
> **not the authority it depicts** — those are two separate statements, and the second is the one a reader of
> a compression is most likely to lose. Named method, protocol, repo, grounding-note, context, and operator
> owners remain authoritative for their claim classes; ASK remains the source-of-intent and authorization
> apex. A disagreement is an audit finding, and it settles nothing by itself: **named owners establish
> current fact; ASK adjudicates whether the system, this compression, or both should change.** No correction
> target follows automatically from a mismatch. The file deliberately omits fast-moving state and local
> mechanism, and confers no permission, scope, conformance obligation, or operative authority.

## What this is

`system-ASK` is a human-governed, AI-mediated **system-building system**: a practice for carrying a source of
intent through definition, realization, selection, governance, and correction — without letting the tools
that execute or critique the work become its normative source.

Three names, one relation:

```text
system-ASK    human ASK, apex-bound to the realization field
ecology-ASK   the bounded realization field of specialised surfaces
method-ASK    the portable methodology, abstracted from the practice and
              reapplied to it
```

The apex sits **inside** the `system-ASK` boundary while remaining **outside** the field it governs —
membership without derivability. That is the whole architecture in one sentence: intent originates at the
apex and is never synthesized upward from the field.
See [`docs/normative-apex.md`](../docs/normative-apex.md) §Membership and derivability are different axes,
and [`examples/system-ASK-bounded-generativity.md`](system-ASK-bounded-generativity.md), which maps the same
practice onto the bounded-generativity primitive at one altitude down.

**Membership in the realization field is functional.** A surface is inside it when its function is
realization — which includes the project repositories and the public surfaces that render and articulate the
work. Being durable, or being public, does not place a surface outside the field; it places it at a
different point within it.

The same relations are projected as a figure:
[`docs/diagrams/method-ASK_system-ASK-hero.html`](../docs/diagrams/method-ASK_system-ASK-hero.html). The
prose and the figure are one delivery unit with two representations. This compression is source of
truth for the figure; neither is authority for the distributed system they depict. The figure is
generated against this accepted claim set, never authored beside it.

## One apex

The governing standard is supplied *to* the execution function and is never legitimated *by* executing it.
Delegation deepens execution; it never raises source-role height. However deep the recursion runs, the
normative apex does not multiply.
→ [`docs/normative-apex.md`](../docs/normative-apex.md) §The apex, §Delegation does not propagate the apex.

## How the upstream layers stand to each other

**Method doctrine, execution protocol, and project realization are not peer layers.** Method doctrine
governs the execution protocol, and the execution protocol governs project realization — an ordered
relation, not a federation. A project does not originate protocol, and the protocol does not originate
method doctrine; each layer supplies the standard the next one operates under.

**Design crosses that stack rather than occupying a rung in it.** `design-system-ASK` is an upstream owner
on its own **visual-inheritance** axis, reaching named consuming surfaces through the routes its owner
declares. Its upstream relation is the separate visual-inheritance axis just named, not a rung in the
method-to-protocol-to-project authority chain; ordering it into that chain would assert a relation no
owner states.
→ [`README.md`](../README.md) §Layer relationship; [`docs/bounded-generativity.md`](../docs/bounded-generativity.md);
[`control-surface/protocol/README.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/control-surface/blob/main/protocol/README.md);
[`control-surface/docs/architecture.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/control-surface/blob/main/docs/architecture.md).

**Conformance runs on a separate axis, and it runs the other way.** `method-ASK` is itself a repository, and
it resolves the execution protocol like any other consumer — so on the conformance axis it is downstream of
`control-surface`, while on the authority axis it remains upstream of it. Collapsing the two axes inverts the
model: it would make the protocol the source of the doctrine it operates under.

## Distributed truth

There is no single document that holds what the system is. Truth is distributed across owners by **aging
rate**: artifacts that *track* state age fast and live with the thing they track; artifacts that *point* at
state age slowly and stay useful across sessions; a carrier that mixes rules, context, and state ages at the
rate of its fastest content. Claims are assigned to owners whose maintenance posture fits their aging rate;
where one carrier mixes rates, it inherits the rate of its fastest-changing limb.
→ [`docs/source-of-truth-and-aging-rate.md`](../docs/source-of-truth-and-aging-rate.md); the execution-side
rule is §Source-of-Truth Boundaries / Aging-Rate Principle in
[`control-surface/protocol/AGENTS.shared.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/control-surface/blob/main/protocol/AGENTS.shared.md);
[`docs/method.md`](../docs/method.md) restates it in one sentence.

This file is subject to that rule, which is why its caveat is the first thing in it.

**A repository's authority is bounded, and the bound is easy to overrun.** A repo is authoritative for
**repo-owned project truth and current repo state** — not for everything the project currently is. Purpose,
workflow, identity, operator state, and delegated domain judgment each have their own owners, and a repo
holding an artifact about one of them has not thereby acquired the claim.
→ `docs/source-of-truth-and-aging-rate.md`.

**Persistence is not authority.** A carrier that survives across sessions has not thereby acquired standing:
a durable operator note, a retained working memory, a cached map — each may persist indefinitely while
remaining non-authoritative operator context, owned by no claim class and settling nothing. Longevity is a
property of storage, not a source of warrant.
→ `docs/source-of-truth-and-aging-rate.md`.

## Plural reasoning, singular mutation

Several differently situated surfaces reason over the same work before any of it is mutated; **one
authorized writer holds each branch or declared mutation aperture.** The discipline is per-aperture, not a
global serialization of unrelated work. The advisor surface holds no write authority and reviews from
outside the execution thread; the execution surface plans and performs authorized work under a single-writer
discipline; ASK relays and adjudicates between them. Occupancy is operational, not architectural — the roles
are slots, not models.
→ [`control-surface/README.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/control-surface/blob/main/README.md)
§Operating Model; [`control-surface/docs/architecture.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/control-surface/blob/main/docs/architecture.md)
§Role Model; `control-surface/protocol/AGENTS.shared.md` §Session Topology / Single-Writer Discipline.

The mutation side is stricter than it looks, and its scope is exact: **a write to shared mutable state must
declare the exclusion guarantee it actually has** — observed-state checks, cooperative exclusion among
participants, or a backend-enforced conditional write — and a caller that needs a stronger guarantee than the
backend provides must get a failure rather than a silent downgrade. Two boundaries hold that rule in place.
It governs writes to **shared mutable state**, not every write, so an ordinary single-writer edit acquires no
concurrency ceremony. And it is a **requirement on such writes**, not a report that every write path in the
system already enforces one. Claiming more than the substrate delivers is the failure the rule exists to
prevent; claiming the rule is already satisfied everywhere would be the same failure at a larger scale.

## Recursive execution

Standing intent reaches work through nested delegation:

```text
standing intent >> execution grant >> derived task intent >> task brief
  >> realization inside the granted aperture
    >> parent verification + selection + integration
      >> program-level authorization + closure
        >> authorized program result
```

Each link changes operational form or granularity without changing normative origin. A parent decomposing an
objective sets subgoals *under* the standard; discovering mid-execution that the standard should change
routes as **candidate correction**, never as silent local revision. Ordinary delegate output is candidate
material until an authorized parent accepts it. Local completion is not program closure. Authority attenuates
downward and never accumulates upward.
→ [`docs/source-of-intent.md`](../docs/source-of-intent.md) §Runtime delegation through the execution span;
[`docs/bounded-generativity.md`](../docs/bounded-generativity.md) §Nested realization fields and recursive
delegation — **nesting creates brackets, not apexes.**

## Governed return

The system leaves its own frame, produces, and brings back what it made — but return is not ascent. Evidence,
critique, and candidate correction travel upward and enter through a **governed absorption gate**: material
is classified before it changes anything, the recipient owns the absorption decision, and nothing becomes
operative by arriving.
→ [`docs/absorption-discipline.md`](../docs/absorption-discipline.md);
[`docs/method.md`](../docs/method.md) §The swing, which additionally names absorption and closure as strokes
of the same maintenance pass; `docs/source-of-intent.md` §Intent repair targets current standing intent,
which holds the load-bearing case: **repair instrumentation measures distance, governance classifies
warrant.** A divergence measure registers movement away from the target; it cannot tell whether that movement
is damage or warranted correction. So a candidate correction is preserved and surfaced to the role authorized
to revise — neither silently adopted nor silently repaired away. *Governed return* is this file's framing;
the absorption limb is owned doctrine.

## How the method itself changes

The three circuits below are all relations *within* a pass. None of them says how the practice's own
abstraction moves, and that movement is the reason `method-ASK` exists as a separate thing from the practice
it came from. **The methodology is abstracted from the practice and reapplied to it, and each pass is tested
against the practice rather than against itself** — adversarial iteration across time, not a fourth loop
inside any single pass. An abstraction validated only against its own prior generation stops tracking the
thing it abstracts.
→ [`docs/method.md`](../docs/method.md) §The swing; [`docs/bounded-generativity.md`](../docs/bounded-generativity.md).

## Raking, and what a raked state can claim

Periodically the practice stops producing and passes back over what it has made: it re-reads the current
owners, restores visible order, and routes what drifted to the owner authorized to correct it. **Producing a
representation does not itself complete that pass.** Completion requires the material findings to receive an
explicit disposition — and a representation is not a disposition. A standing map earns its keep because it
makes distributed relations visible across a field no single document holds; it is dangerous for the same
reason, because it can drift out of agreement with its owners, or be mistaken for the authority it
summarizes.
→ [`docs/method.md`](../docs/method.md).

That danger has a discipline attached. **A claim about a raked state is dated, banded, owner-relative, and
explicit about what it excluded.** A pass covers the sources it actually read, at the altitude it actually
read them, on the day it ran. Reporting it without those qualifiers converts a bounded pass into a stability
claim the pass never established — and a stability claim is exactly what a reader will act on.
→ `docs/method.md`.

```text
Rake basis // 2026-09-09. Band: the stable public relations projected
in this worked example, owner-relative to the public owners cited
inline. Excluded: claims deliberately omitted or held at operator
altitude, live rosters and state, private-instance topology, and the
process and review mechanics by which it was prepared. This records the
bounded basis of the pass. By itself it claims neither a completed rake
nor permanent stability.
```

## Three coupled circuits

```text
PRODUCTION   intent >> grammar / aperture >> authorized realization
             >> selection + closure >> artifact governance >> governed artifact

LEARNING     pressure >> evidence >> critique + classification >> adjudication
             >> a disposition: the correct durable owner and governed propagation,
                OR an explicit nomination, hold, or no-retention

CONTINUITY   fresh context >> bootstrap + index >> named live owners
             >> current work >> lineage preserved >> what remains open
```

The three share one apex and one owner topology. **They do not share one artifact** — which is why no single
document, including this one, can carry the system.

**The learning circuit terminates in a disposition, and propagation is only one of them.** An unearned
generalization becomes an explicit nomination or a hold; material that earns no durable retention is
retained nowhere. A circuit in which everything that enters propagates would describe a system that cannot
refuse, and refusal is the part that keeps the doctrine small.

The circuit names are this file's vocabulary; the mechanisms are owned by the execution protocol
(`control-surface/protocol/AGENTS.shared.md` — plan-before-execute, the exact-scoped-diff gate, review
objects, PR and post-merge discipline; §Learning Disposition; §Verification Claims and §Session-Start
Discipline).

## Stable roles

Roles, not rosters. A new project instantiates an existing role without changing the model.

```text
human ASK        source of intent · authorization · relay · selection ·
                 adjudication · recourse
method-ASK       the portable primitives and their cross-surface relations
control-surface  the execution protocol, review mechanics, and reusable carriers
design-system    three kinds of material, and only three: visual foundations,
                 artifact-inheritance scaffolds, and live surface patterns
project repos    domain truth, implementation, artifacts, a local workflow delta,
                 and the pressure from which protocol rules get discovered —
                 authoritative for repo-owned project truth and current repo state
operator planes  grounding, retrieval maps, coordination, intake, evidence, lineage —
                 they coordinate and preserve, and never own repo truth
```

**Public expression is federated by claim class.** The front door, the publication surfaces, and the public
context scaffold each own what they own; **no owner holds a general public-expression role**, and grouping
them under one heading is this file's orientation lens rather than a relation any owner declares. Read as a
lens it is useful; read as a role it invents an owner.

The front door is a build artifact that renders public identity and proof; it does not become the source of
what it renders.
→ [`ASK/README.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/ASK/blob/main/README.md).

The design-system's three kinds are the owner's own taxonomy. Shared implementation support beneath them —
a helper consumed by several patterns, for instance — is subordinate to those kinds and does not become a
fourth.
→ [`design-system-ASK`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/design-system-ASK).

Within execution, the operating roles are bounded and differently authorized — advisor, executor, critic,
domain authority, delegated subagent. Each role is owned separately; **naming them as one set is this
file's framing.** They are distinguished by **authority, not capability**: a surface that
can do a thing is not thereby permitted to. **Domain authority carries two independent axes** — whether the
expertise sits outside the execution surface, and what its judgment actually binds. Both are declared per
engagement, and neither is fixed by the role's name.
→ [`control-surface/docs/domain-authority-review-protocol.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/control-surface/blob/main/docs/domain-authority-review-protocol.md).

## How material moves

The protocol is resolved, not copied: a shared body resolves verbatim into a consumer's own carrier, so one
rule has one owner and many resolutions.
→ [`control-surface/protocol/README.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/control-surface/blob/main/protocol/README.md).

Design material reaches its consumers by **more than one owner-declared route**. A first-party surface in the
design-system's own repository consumes it by reference; a downstream repository **vendors it at a pinned
commit**; and the owner may declare further routes for particular material. What is universal is the
prohibition, not the mechanism: **nothing is hot-linked at runtime** — no CDN, no runtime fetch — so a
consumer's appearance cannot change underneath it, and re-sync is an explicit act. Where an owner
additionally establishes **byte-identity for a named carrier**, that parity is a property of those named
carriers and is enforced by the owner's own check; it is not a general claim that every vendored file
everywhere is byte-identical to its upstream.
→ the routes and the vendoring rule:
[`design-system-ASK/README.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/design-system-ASK/blob/main/README.md); a named public vendored consumer:
[`ASK/README.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/ASK/blob/main/README.md); the named shared carrier whose byte-identity its owner establishes and enforces:
[`patterns/diagram-static-H/README.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/design-system-ASK/blob/main/patterns/diagram-static-H/README.md).

A **fresh routed handoff** that proceeds through first ingestion and classification distinguishes four
events with different actors and different evidence — routing, feeding, ingestion, disposition — and it may
instead exit before ingestion. **The path is not an inevitability of routing:** an artifact not currently
awaiting first ingestion does not enter it. Absorption is one possible disposition, not the name for all of
them.
→ `control-surface/protocol/AGENTS.shared.md` §Inbound Handoff TBI Marker;
[`docs/intent-artifacts.md`](../docs/intent-artifacts.md).

The visual system is organized in **three tiers**, of which the third is the one that gets *assigned* to a
surface rather than inherited by it. **ASK-the-entity carries ASK Tier 3 by identity. Another surface may
receive that Tier-3 value through explicit local assignment — and the assignment makes it neither ASK-the-entity
nor a member of anything.** What each tier contains, and which surfaces currently hold an assignment, are
owned elsewhere and are not stated here.
→ the assignment relation: [`design-system-ASK/README.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/design-system-ASK/blob/main/README.md);
the ASK-by-identity case: [`ASK/README.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/ASK/blob/main/README.md).

## The wall

Part of the system's context is private and stays private, and the mechanism is worth stating precisely,
because overstating it is the more common error. **Folder separation defines the boundary and makes it
auditable**: a narrow subtree is granted, and everything above it lies outside the grant. **It does not by
itself make the wall structural.** For a named operating surface, the wall is structural only where that
surface is actually confined to the granted subtree. Under that enforced path-scoped grant, the private
parent is outside the surface's reach, and the grant boundary equals the content boundary. Reachability is
not a load instruction, and being reachable does not make a file's contents a conformance obligation.

The public form of this pattern, with the private instance omitted, is published as a scaffold:
[`personal-context-system`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/personal-context-system/tree/stable) — **a
public method artifact, never a private instance**, and never a runtime governor of one. Its generic,
publishable statement of this boundary is
[`templates/context-architecture-decisions.template.md`](https://github.com/apexSolarKiss/personal-context-system/blob/stable/templates/context-architecture-decisions.template.md).
Both links name the `stable` ref deliberately: the default branch moves ahead of what distribution surfaces
point at.

## What this file does not claim

It does not claim that every operation in the system is correctly governed, that every judgment is right, or
that the system proves its own legitimacy. The structure secures answerability **in principle**; whether it
is discharged well in any given case is a separate question, and not one this file can settle. It does not
enumerate repositories, consumers, programs, assignments, or current state — those age at rates this file
deliberately does not track. Where it and a named owner disagree, **the owner establishes the current fact —
and what should change is ASK's to adjudicate: the system, this file, both, or neither.**
